OVERVIEW

THE 2084 SURVIVAL CHALLENGE

The Crisis
The year is 2084. Astro Alliance's manned exploration shuttle, PARIKALP, was sent into
deep space to study a newly discovered cluster of planets. Disaster struck when an unexpected
gravitational pull dragged the shuttle off course, trapping it in the powerful orbit of a mysterious
exoplanet. The main engines are severely damaged. The crew has only enough backup fuel for
one single emergency launch. There are no second chances.
The Problem
To break free from the planet’s gravity and escape into safe space, the crew must fire the engines

to hit the exact Escape Velocity ( 𝑣𝑒 = √ 2𝐺𝑀 / 𝑅 ).

Too slow: The shuttle will crash back down onto the alien surface.

Too fast: The strained backup fuel tanks will explode.

To make matters worse, violent cosmic storms have wrecked the shuttle computer network. The
sensor data showing the planet's mass, radius, and gravity are corrupted, filled with heavy noise,
errors, and missing values. Because raw data is such a mess, standard physics equations cannot be
used directly. In the chaos following the gravitational anomaly, a miscommunication between
mission scientists led to one of the distance parameters being logged in two different units.
Unless this hidden inconsistency is detected and corrected, any prediction could prove fatal.

Your Directive
You are the lead Space Data Scientist in the mission presented on board. Your mission is to build
a reliable predictive pipeline using Python with a very limited amount of time. By training machine
learning models on clean historical planet data taken from the Earth itself, your pipeline must clean
the  noisy  sensor  streams,  reconstruct  the  missing  planetary  features,  and  accurately  predict  the
exact escape velocity.

o  The solution should be reproducible, with clear documentation of preprocessing, feature
