●  There will be two rounds:

1.  Online Submission round
2.  Offline Presentation round

●  Only the teams qualifying in Round 1 will be eligible for Round 2.
●  Round 1: Online Submission Round

■  Submission: Teams must submit their Python Notebook solution and
a Report. Failure to submit either one will lead to disqualification.

■  Total Marks: 100 MARKS

●  70 points for the Python Notebook Solution
●  30 points for the Report

■  Report Requirements: The report must contain the following:

●  Problem Understanding and Approach
●  Methodology and Feature Engineering
●  Results, Analysis, and Conclusions

●  Round 2: Offline Presentation

■  Duration: 20 minutes

●  Presentation Time: 15 minutes
●  Q&A Session: 5 minutes

■  Judgement Criteria:

●  Code clarity
●  Problem-solving approach
●  Quality of presentation

NOTE:  Teams  facing  genuine  and  serious  difficulties  in  attending the offline venue may be permitted to
present online. Such teams must inform the organizers at the earliest possible opportunity and provide the
necessary  details  regarding  their  situation.  The  final  decision  regarding  eligibility  for  an  online
presentation rest solely with Astro-Alliance.
